Drain Cleaning in Silver Spring, MD

Quick answer: If only one fixture is slow, it's a local clog that a plunger, a hand auger or a plumber's cable can usually clear. If several fixtures back up at once, or the basement floor drain gurgles when you flush, the clog is probably in the main sewer line and needs a WSSC-registered drain cleaner.

Fixture clog or main line?

  • One sink or tub slow: a local clog (hair, grease, soap scum).
  • Toilet bubbles when the washer drains, or water comes up the floor drain: a main line problem.
  • Recurring backups every few months: often tree roots in an older clay or cast-iron lateral. A camera inspection is worth paying for.

Why older Silver Spring lines clog

Neighborhoods like Woodside, Forest Glen and Four Corners have mature street trees and many mid-century sewer laterals. Roots work into pipe joints, and kitchen grease builds up on cast iron. Hydro-jetting or root cutting followed by a camera inspection tells you whether you need to clean the line regularly or repair it.

What to avoid

  • Don't pour grease down the drain. WSSC Water runs 'Can the Grease' outreach because grease is a major cause of sewer overflows.
  • Wipes labeled 'flushable' still clog laterals.
  • Don't use chemical drain openers on a fully stopped drain. They sit in the pipe and can damage old metal piping.

Frequently asked questions

How much does drain cleaning cost in Silver Spring?

Prices vary by company and by whether it's a fixture or main line, so get quotes. Main line jobs with a camera inspection cost more than a sink clog. Ask whether the price includes the camera and a video copy.

Is a main sewer clog my problem or WSSC Water's?

It depends where the blockage is. In WSSC Water's area the owner is responsible for the sewer service from the house to the property line, and WSSC handles its side. A registered plumber snakes the line first to find out. See who pays for a sewer backup and sewer line repair.

Does the plumber need to be WSSC-registered?

Yes. WSSC Water requires plumbers and sewer drain cleaners working in Montgomery and Prince George's counties to be registered. Ask for the registration number.
