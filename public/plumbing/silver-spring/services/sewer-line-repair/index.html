Sewer Line Repair & Camera Inspection in Silver Spring, MD

Quick answer: In WSSC Water's area, the homeowner owns the sewer lateral from the house to the property line, and WSSC maintains the section from the property line to the main. Always start with a sewer camera inspection (typically $275–$550 in Silver Spring) and a copy of the video before agreeing to dig. Published 2026 Silver Spring estimates run roughly $1,500–$5,000 for a single-spot repair, $3,300–$16,500 for open-trench lateral replacement, and $4,400–$22,000 for trenchless pipe bursting (or $90–$275/ft for cured-in-place lining). Any underground sewer repair requires a WSSC Water permit pulled by a WSSC-registered master plumber.

Signs your lateral needs repair

  • Backups that return weeks or months after snaking.
  • Tree roots on the cable every time the main drain is cleared (common in older clay and cast-iron laterals in Woodside, Four Corners, Kensington and Takoma Park).
  • Bellied, cracked or offset pipe sections visible on a sewer camera.
  • Soggy patches or sinkholes in the yard along the lateral's path to the street.

What sewer line repair costs in Silver Spring (2026)

  • Published 2026 Silver Spring price ranges vary by method, depth and yard access:
  • Sewer camera inspection: roughly $275–$550 as a standalone diagnostic (sometimes credited toward repair work). Always ask for the video file and distance markings.
  • Spot repair (excavating one broken joint or section): roughly $1,500–$5,000 depending on depth and whether it sits under turf, a sidewalk or a driveway.
  • Full lateral replacement (traditional open trench): roughly $3,300–$16,500 (Plumber Cost 2026 Silver Spring guide), plus landscaping or hardscape restoration.
  • Trenchless pipe bursting or CIPP lining: roughly $4,400–$22,000 for pipe bursting or $90–$275 per linear foot for cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) lining. Trenchless methods cost more per foot upfront but avoid digging a trench across finished yards, retaining walls or driveways.
  • WSSC Water recommends getting at least three written estimates from WSSC-registered master plumbers before authorizing excavation.

Repair options: spot dig, open trench or trenchless

  • Spot repair: best when the camera shows a single crushed joint or root intrusion near a cleanout and the rest of the pipe is sound.
  • Open-trench replacement: required when the old clay, Orangeburg or cast-iron lateral has collapsed or developed a severe belly (sag) that holds standing water.
  • Trenchless pipe bursting: pulls a new seamless HDPE pipe through the old path using two small access pits.
  • CIPP structural lining: cures a smooth epoxy liner inside the existing pipe to seal joints against roots without full excavation.

Permits and WSSC Water rules

  • Silver Spring has no municipal building department. WSSC Water issues all underground plumbing and sewer permits and inspects the work in Montgomery and Prince George's counties.
  • Only a WSSC-registered Master Plumber may excavate or repair underground sewer pipes. Ask for their WSSC license number and a copy of the approved WSSC permit for your records.
  • If the camera shows the break or root blockage is past your property line in WSSC's portion of the lateral, the plumber reports it to WSSC's 24-hour emergency line (301-206-4002) and WSSC repairs its section at no cost to you. See our Silver Spring sewer backup responsibility guide.

Frequently asked questions

How much does sewer line repair or replacement cost in Silver Spring?

Published 2026 Silver Spring estimates put a standalone camera inspection around $275–$550, a single-spot repair around $1,500–$5,000, traditional open-trench replacement around $3,300–$16,500, trenchless pipe bursting around $4,400–$22,000, and CIPP lining around $90–$275 per foot. Depth, length to the property line, utility crossings and driveway restoration drive the spread, so get at least three itemized quotes with camera video.

How do I know whose side the blockage is on?

A WSSC-registered plumber snakes the line to the main and uses a camera with a locator to mark the exact spot and depth. You own from the house to the property line; WSSC Water maintains from the property line to the main (unless the main runs through a right-of-way on your property). If it's on WSSC's side, the plumber reports it to 301-206-4002 and WSSC handles its portion free of charge. Full details: who pays for a sewer backup.

Is trenchless sewer replacement worth it in Silver Spring?

Often, yes, if your lateral runs under a driveway, stone walkway, retaining wall or mature trees. Pipe bursting and CIPP lining avoid tearing up the whole yard. However, if the pipe has collapsed completely or has a deep belly (sag) that traps water, open-trench excavation is usually required to restore proper slope.

Who issues the permit for sewer line replacement in Silver Spring?

WSSC Water—not a city or county building office. Underground sewer repair in Montgomery County requires a WSSC Water permit and inspection, and only a WSSC-registered master plumber can pull the permit.

Should I get a sewer camera inspection before buying an older house?

Yes. Many 1920s–1960s homes in Silver Spring, Takoma Park, Kensington and Wheaton still have original clay or cast-iron sewer laterals under mature trees. A $275–$550 camera inspection before closing can catch root damage or cracks before they become a five-figure replacement.
