Water Heater Repair & Replacement in Silver Spring, MD

Quick answer: A tank water heater usually lasts about 8–12 years. If yours is in that range and leaking from the tank itself, plan to replace it. Leaks from fittings, valves or a bad thermostat or element are usually repairable. In WSSC Water's area (Montgomery and Prince George's counties), replacing a gas water heater requires a WSSC permit, a WSSC-licensed gasfitter and an inspection. A like-for-like electric swap is usually permit-exempt. See our water heater permit guide for the details.

Repair or replace?

  • Repair: no hot water on an electric unit (element or thermostat), a gas unit that won't stay lit (thermocouple or flame sensor), or a dripping relief valve.
  • Replace: rust-colored hot water, rumbling from sediment on an old tank, or water seeping from the bottom of the tank.

Gas, electric or tankless

Many Silver Spring homes have Washington Gas service and gas tank heaters. Condos and some newer homes are all-electric. Tankless units save space but may need a bigger gas line and new venting. Heat-pump water heaters may qualify for utility rebates (check Pepco's or Washington Gas's current programs).

Permits and code

  • Gas water heater: WSSC Water permit (the pre-paid Short Form permit is $180 as of July 2026, inspection included), installed by a WSSC-licensed gasfitter, inspected within 10 business days. Homeowners can't install gas appliances themselves.
  • Electric, like-for-like: WSSC's code exempts replacing a residential electric water heater from the permit requirement, as long as no concealed piping is replaced and no pipes or valves are rearranged.
  • Tankless, relocation or new venting: treat it as permitted work and ask the plumber to confirm in writing.
  • Keep the permit and the passed inspection. The installer's required warranty runs from final inspection approval. Full details: Do you need a permit to replace a water heater?

Frequently asked questions

How much does a water heater replacement cost in Silver Spring?

In public r/MontgomeryCountyMD threads from 2026, homeowners reported paying roughly $1,600–$2,200 for a standard 40–50 gallon tank swap. Venting problems such as a chimney liner can push it much higher. Make sure the quote includes the WSSC permit fee for a gas unit, haul-away, and any expansion tank or drain pan.

How long does a water heater replacement take?

A like-for-like tank swap is often done the same day. Switching to tankless or moving the unit takes longer.

Why is my water heater leaking from the top?

Usually a loose inlet or outlet fitting, or the relief valve. Both are often repairable. A leak from the bottom of the tank usually means the tank has failed.
