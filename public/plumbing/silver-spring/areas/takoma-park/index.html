Frequently asked questions

Do plumbers in Takoma Park need to be WSSC-registered?

On the Maryland side, yes. Plumbers must be WSSC Water-registered. Addresses in Takoma, DC fall under DC Water and DC licensing.

Who do I call for a sewer backup in Takoma Park?

Call WSSC Water's 24-hour emergency line (301-206-4002) and a registered plumber. The plumber determines whether the blockage is on your side or WSSC's side.

What should I do first in a plumbing emergency in Takoma Park?

Shut off the main water valve for a burst pipe or major leak, and stop running water if sewage is backing up. Then call a plumber. Our emergency plumbing checklist covers the first 10 minutes.

Do I need a permit for a water heater or sewer line repair in Takoma Park?

Replacing a gas water heater ($180 WSSC Short Form permit) or repairing an underground water or sewer line requires a WSSC Water permit and inspection. Like-for-like electric water heater swaps are usually exempt. See our water heater permit guide and sewer line repair guide.
