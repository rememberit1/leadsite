Before you hire any plumber in Montgomery County

  • Ask for their WSSC Water registration number. It's required for plumbing and sewer work in Montgomery and Prince George's counties.
  • For underground water or sewer line work, confirm a registered master plumber will pull a WSSC permit.
  • Get the price in writing before work starts, and ask for camera video on any sewer diagnosis.
